# Signup Flow CRO Guide

## Core Strategy
The guide emphasizes **minimizing friction** through three main principles: reduce required fields to essentials only, demonstrate value before requesting commitment, and eliminate uncertainty about what comes next.

## Essential Optimization Areas

**Field Selection**: Only collect data genuinely needed at signup. Every field reduces conversion—defer company, role, and similar details to post-signup progressive profiling.

**Authentication Options**: Social auth (Google, Apple, Microsoft) often outperforms email signup and should be prominently positioned, especially for B2B audiences.

**Multi-Step Flows**: When needed, follow a "progressive commitment pattern"—starting with just email, then adding password/name, with optional customization questions last.

## Measurement Focus
Track completion rates by field, identify where users abandon forms, and distinguish mobile vs. desktop performance. Specific error handling and inline validation significantly improve outcomes.

## Testing Priorities
High-impact experiments include: reducing field count, testing SSO prominence, optimizing button copy ("Get Started" vs. "Create Account"), and validating whether credit cards are required for trials.
