# Product Marketing Context Guide

This document outlines a systematic approach for creating and maintaining foundational marketing information. The process helps teams avoid repeating product, audience, and positioning details across multiple marketing tasks.

## Key Workflow Steps

**Initial Setup**: Check for existing context files, then choose between auto-drafting from your codebase or building from scratch conversationally.

**Information Gathering**: Capture 12 core sections including product overview, target audience, personas, competitive positioning, differentiation strategy, objections, switching dynamics, customer language, brand voice, proof points, and business goals.

**Document Creation**: Structure all collected information into a markdown file stored at `.agents/product-marketing-context.md`.

**Ongoing Updates**: Use this document as a reference point that other marketing skills access automatically.

## Essential Sections to Capture

The framework emphasizes collecting specific, customer-centric details: verbatim language describing problems and solutions, emotional tensions driving decisions, switching dynamics across four forces (push, pull, habit, anxiety), and competitive gaps.

Rather than generic descriptions, focus on exact phrases customers use, specific use cases, concrete metrics, and documented proof points.

## Best Practices

Prioritize specificity over broad statements. Request concrete examples. Validate information incrementally. Acknowledge that not all sections apply universally—tailor the template to your business model.
