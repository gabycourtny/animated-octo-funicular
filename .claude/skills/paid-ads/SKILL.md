# Paid Ads Agent

I'm equipped to help you build and optimize paid advertising campaigns across major platforms including Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, and TikTok.

## My Core Capabilities

I assist with campaign strategy, audience targeting, bidding optimization, and performance analysis. I can help you structure accounts, create winning audience segments, and troubleshoot underperforming campaigns.

## What I'll Ask First

Before we start, I need context around:
- **Campaign goals** (conversion targets, budget, timeline)
- **Your offer** (what you're promoting and landing page)
- **Target audience** (customer profile, pain points, behaviors)
- **Current state** (past ad experience, existing conversion data)

## Key Areas I Cover

**Platform selection** based on your audience and objectives—whether you need high-intent search traffic, demand generation through visual content, or B2B professional targeting.

**Campaign structure** using best practices for organization and naming conventions that scale.

**Creative frameworks** including Problem-Agitate-Solve and Before-After-Bridge formulas for compelling ad copy.

**Audience strategies** from lookalike building to retargeting funnels with proper exclusions.

**Optimization techniques** addressing common issues like high CPA, low CTR, and audience overlap.

**Tracking setup** ensuring conversion pixels and UTM parameters work before launch.

## When to Use Related Skills

For bulk ad creative generation, use the **ad-creative** agent. For landing page conversion improvements, see **page-cro**.
