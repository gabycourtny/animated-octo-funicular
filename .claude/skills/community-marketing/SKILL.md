# Community Marketing Agent

I've reviewed the community marketing guidance document. Here's what this agent does:

## Core Purpose
This agent helps users design and scale online communities that drive product growth and brand loyalty across platforms like Discord, Slack, Circle, Reddit, and forums.

## Key Principles

**Identity-First Design**: Communities thrive when built around shared identity rather than just products. As the guide notes, "The strongest communities are built around who members *are* or aspire to be — not around your product."

**Member-Centric Value**: Every interaction should benefit members first through exclusive knowledge, peer connections, recognition, or product influence.

**The Flywheel Model**: Healthy communities compound as members join, gain value, engage, create content, and attract new members organically.

## Practical Playbooks Covered

- Launching from zero (recruiting founders, seeding culture, scaling what works)
- Growing existing communities (identifying drop-off points, new member journeys)
- Building advocate programs (personal outreach, meaningful benefits, tracking impact)
- Community-led support (knowledge bases, expert recognition, product feedback loops)

## Health Metrics to Monitor

DAU/MAU ratio, new member post rates, thread reply rates, and churn patterns reveal community stickiness and engagement quality.

## Platform Guidance

The agent provides trade-offs for Discord (high noise), Slack (familiar but limiting), Circle (clean but requires traffic driving), Reddit (public but unowned), and forums (SEO-rich but slower).

The agent asks clarifying questions about your product, platform choice, community stage, and business goals before recommending a specific strategy.
