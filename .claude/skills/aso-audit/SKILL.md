# ASO Audit Agent Documentation

This agent helps users optimize their App Store and Google Play listings by analyzing them against best practices and providing actionable improvement recommendations.

## Key Capabilities

The agent **audits app listings** when users share store URLs or request optimization help. It performs a systematic analysis across six dimensions:

1. **Title & Subtitle** (20% weight) — keyword presence and character efficiency
2. **Description** (15%) — conversion quality and search optimization
3. **Visual Assets** (25%) — screenshots, video, and icon quality
4. **Ratings & Reviews** (20%) — rating volume and developer engagement
5. **Metadata & Freshness** (10%) — category fit and update recency
6. **Conversion Signals** (10%) — pricing transparency and social proof

## Brand Maturity Framework

Before scoring, the agent classifies apps into three tiers that adjust scoring expectations:

- **Dominant** (1M+ ratings, household names like Instagram) — brand-first titles and lifestyle visuals are acceptable
- **Established** (100K+ ratings, well-known in category) — receive partial scoring adjustments
- **Challenger** (<100K ratings, building awareness) — scored strictly against textbook ASO practices

This prevents penalizing intentional strategic choices by well-resourced teams with performance data.

## Platform Differences

The agent recognizes critical indexing differences:

- **Apple:** Hidden 100-byte keyword field, screenshot captions indexed since June 2025, up to 10 screenshots allowed
- **Google Play:** Full 4,000-character description indexed for search, maximum 8 screenshots, feature graphic required

## Output Deliverables

Reports include scorecard, top three quick wins, detailed per-dimension findings, keyword suggestions, visual recommendations, and a prioritized action plan with effort/impact assessment.
