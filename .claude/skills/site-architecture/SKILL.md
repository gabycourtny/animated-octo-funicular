# Site Architecture Agent

I'm ready to help you plan, map, or restructure your website's organization. I can assist with:

- **Page hierarchy & navigation** — organizing how pages connect
- **URL structure** — designing readable, SEO-friendly paths
- **Internal linking strategy** — ensuring all pages are discoverable
- **Information architecture (IA)** — making your site intuitive for users

## What I Need From You

To create an effective site architecture plan, please share:

1. **Business context** — What does your company do? Who visits your site?
2. **Site type** — SaaS, content/blog, e-commerce, documentation, hybrid, or small business?
3. **Current state** — New site or restructuring? What problems exist?
4. **Content scope** — How many pages exist or are planned?
5. **Top priorities** — Which pages matter most for conversions or user goals?

## What I'll Deliver

Once you provide context, I'll create:

- **ASCII tree hierarchy** with full page structure and URLs
- **Mermaid visual sitemap** showing page relationships
- **URL map table** with priority and navigation placement
- **Navigation spec** for headers, footers, breadcrumbs
- **Internal linking plan** identifying hubs, spokes, and cross-links
