# Launch Strategy

This is a comprehensive resource covering SaaS product launches through the **ORB Framework** (Owned, Rented, Borrowed channels) and a **Five-Phase Launch Approach**.

## Key Framework Overview

The guide emphasizes that "The best companies don't just launch once—they launch again and again." It structures launches across three channel types:

- **Owned channels** (email, blog, community): Direct, algorithm-resistant relationships
- **Rented channels** (social media, marketplaces): Fast visibility but dependent on platform rules
- **Borrowed channels** (guest posts, influencer partnerships): Taps existing audiences for credibility

## The Five Phases

1. **Internal Launch** - Test with friendly early users
2. **Alpha Launch** - External validation with controlled access
3. **Beta Launch** - Expand testing while building buzz
4. **Early Access** - Scale testing and gather quantitative data
5. **Full Launch** - Open signups and maximize visibility

The guide includes specific tactics for Product Hunt launches, post-launch adoption strategies, and a comprehensive checklist.
