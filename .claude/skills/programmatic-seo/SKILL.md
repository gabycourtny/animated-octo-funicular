# Programmatic SEO

Programmatic SEO involves creating SEO-optimized pages at scale using templates and data. This approach targets multiple keywords or locations through systematic page generation.

## Key Principles

The strategy emphasizes that "every page must provide value specific to that page" rather than simply swapping variables. Success depends on maximizing unique content across pages.

Data quality matters significantly. The strongest approach uses proprietary information you've created, followed by product-derived data from users, user-generated content, licensed data, and finally public information.

## Technical Best Practices

For URL structure, "use subfolders, not subdomains" to consolidate domain authority. For example: `yoursite.com/templates/resume/` works better than `templates.yoursite.com/resume/`.

Quality surpasses quantity—the framework recommends 100 well-crafted pages over 10,000 thin ones.

## The 12 Playbooks

Common patterns include templates, comparisons, location-based pages, integrations, directories, and glossaries. Selection depends on your data type and product.

## Critical Safeguards

Avoid thin content, keyword cannibalization, and pages without genuine search demand. Google penalties apply to doorway pages, keyword stuffing, and duplicate content.

Pre-launch checklists should verify unique value, proper technical SEO, internal linking, and indexation strategy. Post-launch monitoring tracks rankings, traffic, and engagement metrics.
