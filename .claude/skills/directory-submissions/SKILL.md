# Directory Submissions Guide

I've read the comprehensive directory submissions playbook. Here's the actionable summary:

## Core Purpose
Directory submissions create a **foundation layer** for distribution by passing dofollow backlinks into your marketing pages, creating discovery surface area, and getting your product cited by AI engines (which converts 6–27× better than traditional search traffic).

## Three Non-Negotiable Rules

1. **Build destination pages first** — directories are worthless without landing pages (alternatives, use cases, templates) ready to receive link equity.

2. **Foundation readiness** — your product needs pricing, privacy policy, logos, 5–8 screenshots, demo video, and GEO-optimized pages with single H1 and schema markup before submitting anywhere.

3. **Vary positioning per tier** — "copy-paste the same description everywhere" destroys value. Each directory tier (startup vs. SaaS vs. AI) needs distinct framing.

## Submission Strategy

**Tier 1 (Launch week):** Product Hunt, BetaList, HN Show HN (~15 directories)
**Tier 2 (Week 1+):** AlternativeTo, G2, Capterra, SaaSHub (~15)
**Tier 3 (Week 1–3):** TAAFT, Futurepedia, Toolify, aitools.inc (~25)
**Tier 4 (if applicable):** Agent/MCP registries like Glama (~10)

## Critical Win: Product Hunt Execution

Launch at 12:01 AM PT on Tuesday/Wednesday/Thursday. The 2026 algorithm weights comment quality over upvotes. **Never ask for upvotes—ask for feedback.** You need 50+ supporters in the first 2 hours to trigger algorithmic distribution.

## Reviews Matter

G2/Capterra are worthless without reviews. Run the "10-in-30 protocol": identify 20 power users, send direct review links (no landing pages), offer modest incentives. Target 50% conversion = 10 reviews by day 30.

## What Actually Converts

Directories are foundational infrastructure, not the whole strategy. Real growth comes from:
- Alternative pages (5–15% conversion)
- Use-case targeting pages
- Template galleries (Typeform generated $3M/year LTV from this)
- Honest "best of" category articles
