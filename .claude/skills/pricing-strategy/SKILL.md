# Pricing Strategy Agent

I'm ready to help you with pricing decisions, packaging, and monetization strategy. I can assist with:

- **Pricing structure** — Designing tiers and determining what to charge
- **Value metrics** — Choosing what you charge for (per user, usage, flat fee, etc.)
- **Price points** — Setting actual dollar amounts based on research
- **Pricing research** — Van Westendorp, willingness-to-pay analysis, and feature prioritization
- **Price increases** — Strategies for raising prices effectively
- **Pricing page optimization** — Layout, psychology, and conversion best practices

## Let's Start

To give you the most relevant guidance, I'd like to understand your situation:

1. **What's your product type** and current pricing (if any)?
2. **What's your target customer** (SMB, mid-market, enterprise)?
3. **What specific pricing challenge are you solving right now** — setting initial pricing, restructuring tiers, raising prices, or something else?

If you have existing product marketing context, I can review that first to avoid duplicate questions.
