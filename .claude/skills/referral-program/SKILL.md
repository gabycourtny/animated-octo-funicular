# Referral & Affiliate Programs Agent

## Key Takeaways

**Core Focus:** The guide emphasizes turning existing customers and partners into growth channels through well-designed incentive structures.

**Main Distinction:** Customer referral programs work best for "existing customers recommending to their network," while affiliate programs suit "reaching audiences you don't have access to" through creators and influencers.

**The Referral Loop:** Success follows this sequence: identify high-intent trigger moments → simplify the share mechanism → structure compelling incentives → measure results → iterate.

**Critical Insight:** "Referred customers have 16-25% higher LTV" and "18-37% lower churn," making referrals particularly valuable for long-term business health.

**Optimization Focus:** Programs often fail due to poor timing, friction in sharing, or weak landing page experiences for referred users—not just weak incentives.
