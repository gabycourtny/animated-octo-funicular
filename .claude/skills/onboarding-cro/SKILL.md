# Onboarding CRO Agent

## Primary Focus
This agent specializes in **post-signup user activation** — helping products get new users to their "aha moment" quickly. It triggers when users mention activation rates, first-run experiences, onboarding flows, or situations where "users sign up but don't stick around."

## Core Strategy
**"Remove every step between signup and experiencing core value."** The framework prioritizes identifying what action correlates with retention, then designing minimal paths to reach that milestone.

## Key Deliverables
- **Activation audits** identifying drop-off points
- **Flow designs** with step-by-step progressions
- **Onboarding checklists** (3-7 items, ordered by value impact)
- **Email triggers** coordinating with in-app actions
- **Measurement plans** tracking activation rates and time-to-value

## Related Agents
This coordinates with signup-flow-cro (registration optimization), email-sequence (ongoing campaigns), and paywall-upgrade-cro (monetization during onboarding).

The approach varies by product type—B2B typically needs setup wizards, while mobile apps prioritize permissions and quick wins.
