# AI SEO Guide Summary

This comprehensive guide covers optimizing content for AI search engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Google AI Overviews.

## Key Takeaways

**The Core Difference:** Traditional SEO ranks pages; AI SEO gets your content cited as sources in AI-generated answers.

**Three Optimization Pillars:**
1. **Structure** — Make content easily extractable (definition blocks, comparison tables, step-by-step guides)
2. **Authority** — Build citation-worthiness through statistics (+37% boost), expert attribution (+25-30%), and proper sourcing
3. **Presence** — Appear across platforms where AI looks, including Wikipedia, Reddit, and review sites

**Critical Stats:**
- AI Overviews appear in ~45% of Google searches
- Optimized content gets cited 3x more often than non-optimized
- "Cite sources" and "Add statistics" provide the largest visibility boosts

## Essential Actions

- **Audit current visibility** across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google for your priority queries
- **Verify AI bot access** — ensure your robots.txt allows GPTBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot, and others
- **Add machine-readable files** like `/pricing.md` so AI agents can parse your information
- **Implement schema markup** (Article, HowTo, FAQ, Product) for 30-40% higher visibility
- **Monitor monthly** using tools like Otterly AI or Peec AI

**Avoid:** Keyword stuffing (reduces visibility by 10%), gated content, missing dates, generic claims without data, and blocking AI crawlers.
