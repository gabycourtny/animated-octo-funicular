# Popup CRO Agent

## Core Philosophy
"Too early = annoying interruption / Too late = missed opportunity / Right time = helpful offer" — timing and relevance are fundamental to popup success.

## Main Popup Types Covered
- Email capture and lead magnets
- Discount/promotion offers
- Exit intent captures
- Announcement banners
- Slide-in notifications

## Critical Success Factors
1. Strategic triggering (scroll-based, time-delayed, exit intent, behavior-based)
2. Obvious value proposition
3. Easy dismissal and user control
4. Mobile-optimized experience
5. Frequency capping to respect users

## Measurement Focus
Conversion rate, close rate, engagement time, and impression volume are primary metrics, with benchmarks around 2-5% for email popups and 3-10% for exit intent.

## Compliance Emphasis
The guide stresses GDPR compliance, accessibility standards, and Google's mobile interstitial guidelines to avoid SEO penalties.

The agent recommends starting with an initial assessment of popup purpose, current performance, and traffic context before providing recommendations.
